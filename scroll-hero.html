That is the part the original component could not do. It pinned document.body on mount and never let go, so this text was unreachable — scrolling only ever moved the video.

Here the lock releases the moment your gesture pushes past the end of the video, and re-engages if you scroll back up into the hero. Try it: scroll up and the doors close again.

It also lets people out.

Keyboard scrolling works while locked — arrows, Page Up / Page Down, Space, Home and End all scrub. The Skip intro button is a deliberate escape hatch, and anyone with prefers-reduced-motion set never gets locked at all: the video parks on its final frame and the page scrolls normally.
